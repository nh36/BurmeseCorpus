JOUR. BURMA RESEARCH SOC.
To
XXXI, 1, 1941.
INDEXING BURMESE NAMES
Dear Sir,
The Hon. Editor, Journal of the Burma Research Society,
A correspondent of mine has raised the interesting and important
question of the best method of indexing Burmese names. In the early issues
of the Journal, the List of Members was indexed under the first name in
each case, so that, for example, "Ba Maung" appeared under "B". After
two or three years this method was abandoned in favour of indexing under
the final name, so that "Ba Maung"
Ba Maung" came to be placed under "M".
Government, in its Civil List, indexes in the latter manner. Harvey, how-
ever, in his History of Burma, prefers the former method.
I should be grateful if you could publish this letter in the Journal so
that the views of Members may be ascertained.
Rangoon: 12th March, 1941.
Yours faithfully,
B. R. Pearn.
