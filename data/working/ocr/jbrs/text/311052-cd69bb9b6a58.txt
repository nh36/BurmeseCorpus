JOUR, BURMA RESEARCH SOC.
CORRESPONDENCE
KING MINDON'S BANKER
To
XXXI, 1, 1941.
Dear Sir,
The Secretary, Burma Research Society.
I shall be grateful if you can give me any information about U Pho,
the Maha Yatha Thahte or Royal Banker of King Mindon.
According to some information in my possession I gather that King
Mindon conferred upon this man U Pho the title of Maha Yatha Thahte
and advanced him money to lend to the people at the rate of five rupees per
hundred per month. The Thahte was said to have died of fits of insanity.
So far I have not been able to find anything about him in any historical
book. I shall therefore be grateful if you can refer me to any books or
information about this personage.
Yours faithfully,
Tun Myint.
Magwe: 24th February, 1941.
