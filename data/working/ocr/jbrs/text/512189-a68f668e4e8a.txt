OBITUARY NOTE
Maha Tharay Sithu U Ba Lwin
U Ba Lwin, a staunch nationalist and a promi-
nent educationist, holder of the title Maha Tharay
Sithu, was born in 1892 to U Po Thein and Daw
Khin in Rangoon. He gained the B. A. degree with
English Honours from the University of Rangoon and
Diploma in Education from the London Day Train-
ing College. He began his career as Deputy Inspect-
or of Schools and later worked as a journalist at
the Sun. One of the leaders of the national educa-
tion movement following the 1920 University Boycott,
he took appointment as head and founder of the
Myoma National High School in Rangoon. He was
a Senator to the Burma Senate. He led a study mission
to Japan during the World War II. After the War
and Independence in 1948, he led educational and
cultural delegation on a number of occasions, includ-
ing a Burmese delegation to the UNESCO Conference
in Paris. He was appointed Ambassador to Ceylon
in 1953. He has been a member of the Executive
Conmittee of the Burma Research Society for several
years. Died on April 7, 1968, at the age of seventy six.
