BURMESE LINE SKETCHES
In March 1961 Professor U Wun brought back this Parapuik from U Maung Maung Tin,
Lecturer, Burmese Department, Mandalay University. Unfortunately we cannot say who
did these drawings and to which period they belong. Nevertheless they bear close
resemblence to six paintings collected at Mandalay in 1874 by one Dr. Dwyer who handed
them over to Charles Alexander Gordon to be published in his book Our Trip to Burmah
(23 December 1874-23 February 1875) (London. Bailliere, Tindall and Cox. King William
Street, Strand). For the sake of comparison we reproduce them here along with the
eighteen photographic reproductions from the Parapuik. Probably this Parapuik is meant
to serve as a manual on drawing for beginners in about the last quarter of the 19th
century. We are greatly indebted to U Tin U, Editor, Department of Translation and
Publications, University of Rangoon for kindly taking protographs of the Parapuik and to
U Maung Maung Tin for giving us permission to reproduce the Parapuik in the Journal.
