"Eastward" in line 10, page 166 is a careless mistake of mine. I

the Salween on its left or eastern bank, then a difficulty arises to
reconcile the statements about his return to Ayut'ia and about his
reaching the Sittang river soon after leaving Muang K'reng. Gyaing is
very far away from the Sittang river, which is very much nearer Pegu
than Gyaing. To go to Ayut'ia he would have to go eastward, and to

I put in the word "westward" though not in the
original, to reconcile this statement with the other, namely that he
reached the Sittang river after leaving Muang K`reng. The account
given by His Royal Highness Prince Damrong in his "Our Wars with
the Burmese" and by Mr. W. A. R. Wood in his "History of Siam," is
more probable. According to His Royal Highness and Mr. Wood,
Prince Phra Naresuan, after declaring "that he renounced, on behalf of
his father, Siam's allegiance to Burma", proceeded to Hanthawaddy and
laid siege to the city first before returning to Ayut'ia. If such was the
case, he would have to cross the Sittang on his way back. Therefore,
the account given by His Royal Highness and Mr. Wood is more
probable and definite then that given in the Siamese History. (See
Prince Damrong's “Our Wars with the Burmese" page 94, and Wood's
"History of Siam" pages 132–133).
