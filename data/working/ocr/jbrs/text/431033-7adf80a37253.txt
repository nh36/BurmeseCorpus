THE PLACE OF VIETNAMESE LANGUAGE IN THE
BUILDING OF A NEW CULTURE OF THE VIETNAMESE PEOPLE
by
Dang Thai Mai
Mr. Chairman Esteemed Delegates, Ladies and Gentlemen,
I feel a great honour to have the opportunity to speak to-day on the subject "The
place of Vietnamese language in the building of a new culture of the Vietnamese people".
Dear friends,
The language of a people is not a mere question of form; also it is not simply a
means. We sould say in this matter, the forms implies the contents and the means by
itself constitutes the end too. The language of a country is always and clearly adorned
with a national character, and on the process of serving the exchange of scientific ideology,
the language itself has been trained and formed to be able to become rich and exact.
Fifteen years ago, when we had not yet gained our independence, the Vietnamese
language took a very dim position at schools as well as in the works of scientific study. The
Vietnamese language was only taught at schools in primary classes. Few people thought of
using it as a mean for the propagation of sciences and techniques. It was of the opinion
that the Vietnamese language has no possibilities to become the voice of science. For this
reason, it could not be developed. To-day, that situation altered completely.
The cultural policy of the Government of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam and of
President Ho-Chi-Minh who are leading the Revolution to achieve national liberation,
reunification of the country and socialist construction, has always impregnated with this
spirit: all achievements of the Revolution are to serve the people. Culture and science in
Vietnam should first and foremost belong to the Vietnamese people. Therefore, the populari-
zation of science as well as raising of scientific level should be done with the national means
and form, that is the language of the Vietnamese people.
Right from the first days of its fouding in 1945, and through the period of arduous and
dangerous activities of an illegal party under the colonialist rule from 1930-44, the leading
revolutionary party and President Ho-Chi-Minh were always interested in widely propagating
the learning of written Vietnamese among the people's masses. Our Vietnamese revolu-
tionaries encouraged the wide circulation of books and newspapers in the countryside. The
Association for Propagation of National Language founded in 1938 carried its works
actively from North to South, even during the World War II from 1938–45. Legal
and underground revolutionary publications in that period were intended mainly to instil new
ideas into intellectual circles, but did not forget another aim to educate the masses.
If You Gentlemen, think that, according to figures given by French scholars, the percentage
of illiterate people in Vietnam at that time was over 95% of the population, you realize all
the more the great scope and the difficulties of the movement.

Then in 1942, three years before the August Revolution of 1945, the Vietnamese revo-
lutionaries made public their cultural programme which set forth three leading principles of
their cultural policy: national. scientific and popular. The cultural revolution in Vietnam
has been always closely linked with the political revolution. It aims at serving the people,
serving the national liberation. It follows a scientific way of developing all good elements
of the traditional culture, and at the same time, assimilating the sound elements of all pro-
gressive peoples in the world.
The August revolution brought to the Vietnamese people equality in all fields of activi-
ties political, economic and cultural. Together with the right to vote, the Vietnamese people
enjoy the right of work and study. And this is a fundamental point of the first constitution
of our country. Immediately after the establishment of our government, President Ho-Chi-
Minh signed a decree ordering the setting up of the Mass Education Department.
A great
seething and continuos movement gained momentum under the guidance of the people's
power. Throughout the country, all illiterates started schooling, all literats started teaching.
From primary schools to the universities, the Vietnamese language has become a medium for
the propagation of science, of natural and social science. The entire Vietnames people
warmly welcome and supported President Ho-Chi-Minh's appeal and carried out this policy
of the government. Everybody felt that "the use of one's tongue in one's mouth for express-
ing one's ideals and sentiments" was indeed an invaluable right which was denied to the
Vietnamese people for eighty long years under the colonial regime.
The foresaid cultural policy based on three fundamental principles were applied under
extremely difficult conditions during the nine years of nation wide resistance. The Vietnam-
ese people continued to study in the midst of war. They studied in schools, in factories, in
all over the conntryside. Workers and soldiers studied to master crafts, cadres and the
population to understand the government policy and to carry them out, pupils and students
to heighten their general and technical knowledge. The Vietnamese language, became a
medium for the propagation of science and knowledge among the masses. In the army, the
peasants were able to handle the machine-gun, the young workers in the foctories to deal with
modern machinery, and the farmers, to discuss the government's policies and world events.
Good phophylactics were continously applied all through our nine years of resistance war, and
allowed no occurrence of any epidemic whatever. Stress laid on correct political understand-
ing resulted in a purification of morals as compared with the pre-revolution years, Indeed, it
was a more active and useful life altogether. A new type of heros appeared in the army as
well as in the factories and in the countryside. They were armymen, workers, peasants, intellec-
tuals who thought of renovation and improvement in their daily work, may it be a different
method of analysis, of machining or of ploughing. Miracles in science occured when truth
came within the reach of the masses. It was a truth which led the way, a dynamic force.
The victory of the Vietnamese resistance is first and foremost the victory of a national, scien-
tific and popular culture in the struggle for Truth, for Humanity, for National Independence,
for Peace and Democracy and for the Happiness of the People.
After the signing of the Geneva Agreement in 1954, in the plan of building a strong and
prosperous Vietnam, to serve as basis for the struggle for the peaceful national reunification,
cultural development is more than ever necessary. We need technical cadres, we need skilled
and able workers in every field of sciences and techniques. Indeed, we need cooperative mem-

In the course of 1959, the publishing house for education published 7,689,646 books for

year 37 kinds of books on the study of the Vietnamese literature, of the world literature.
history...with a total number of 153,440 copies. I could not possibly mention here the

Youth Publishing House, the Pioneer Publishing House, the Literary Publishing House, the

to mention the publication of two verse collections that scored great records never known

1955, its circulation reached up to 300,000 copies. Another collection of poems belongs to
our well-known contemporary poet To-Huu. This book published twice in 1959, and the
number of circulation reached up to 120,000 copies.
The need arises that national culture should be developed in consonnanee with the
people's needs in economice development, and at the same time, the people's scientific level
should be raised on the basis of the propagation of scientific knowlege among the broad
masses of the people.

Early this year (1959), the Government of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam set up
the National Scientific Board with the view to guiding the development of science according
to the state plan. Scientific researches are being carried out in the various institutes, uni-
versities, and technical schools with the purpose of acquiring gradually and systematically the
knowledge of modern science, and at the same time propagating it among the people. In
this respect, the Vietnam Association for the Propagation of Sciences and Techniques was
founded. The purpose of the Association is to unite all those scientists who have sat for
themselves the task of contributing to the heightening of the nation's science and culture level,
and to the propagation of science among the masses. For this purpose, the National Scien-
tific Research Board and the Association for the Propagation of Sciences and Techniques
recently founded have issued two monthly magazines: "The Applied Science" intended for the
broad masses with a montly circulation of 35,000 copies, and “Information on Scientific
Activities" intened for readers of a higher level with a monthly circulation of 4,500 copies.
Dear friends,
In the processes of endeavours of the Vietnamese people to develop a new national
culture, the Vietnamese language has carved its own position. It has proved to be capable
of becoming a suitable instrument for scientific analysis. Along with the changes in the
material and cultural life of the present Vietnamese society, Vietnamese has become definite-
ly more complicated than before. That is why, so many new problems in the domain of
linguistic have cropped up posing many new tasks before our scholars. The researches in
the provinces of sounds, meaning, the standardization of the Vietnamese lauguage, the
compilation of a new Vietnamese dictionary and a naw Vietnamese grammar are important
items on the order of the day. These are the immediate tasks for Vitnamese linguists and
literature scholars. Under the leadership of the National Scientific Reserrch Board, our
Institute of Literature and Linguistic are working hard to fulfil these tasks. The leader of
our National Scientific Research Board, Deputy Prime Minister Truong-Chinh who is also
an eminent scholar in social sciences and a matured writer, always attaches particular atten-
tion to the preservation of the inherent clarity of the Vietnamese language while endeavouring
to render our mother tongue richer and more precise. Our scholars are deeply conscious of
the importance of their task and also are fully aware of its extreme complicacy. In the do-
main of science, we connot always say: to pose the problem in to solve it". However, the
important thing is to have a correct line of action to be determined to carry though one's.
studies of concrete realities, to ponder seriously on one's experiences and to study from the
experiences of fellow scientific research workers and of all the peoples in the world.
