for one thing that the most important influence exerted by the Moon upon
the surface of the Earth is the producing of tides. But the question may
also be raised whether the tides are not influenced by other heavenly bodies.
The laws of tides given us by mathematicians permit us to detemine this.
The nearest planet to the Earth is Venus which, compared with the Moon,
has a mass about 70 times as great, but is 100 times farther from the
Earth. Hence, the tide producing force of Venus as compared with that of
the Moon is given by the fraction
In other words the tide-
producing force of Venus is less than one ten-thousandth of the moon.
If the Moon brought about tides having a range of 100 feet, we might
expect a Venusian tide of something like one-tenth of an inch. Actually,
therefore, the tidal effect of Venus upon the Earth is practically nil accord-
ing to the mathematical formula which has been propounded for our guid-
ance. This would mean that Venus in conjunction with the Moon produces
no effect upon life. The Burmese Astrology holds otherwise as it apparent-

exerted by Venus added to the enormous tide-producing powers of the

Now having explained the meaning of the digit nine I may take the
liberty of adding this number to one of my two series so as to dispose of
the whole 9 digits of our system of numerals. I might appropriately add this
to the lower row of the two i.e. 2718 which thus becomes 27189. Even
with this addition of the number 9 to the second of the two series my claim
that these two series of numbers i.e. 6543 and 2718 are mutually exclusive
still holds. The continued product of 6543 is equal to 360 which in terms
of the philosophy of numbers is zero or the circle representing the total
number of the digits, and is considered the perfect number because it brings
all things back to unity. The continued product of the number 27189 or
1008 is caught within the circle and the ratio of 360 to 1008 is 2.8
which in the astrological sense is the same as 28. Here we see that this
ratio 28 is the sum of the digits 1234567. This is how there are only 7
days in a week and how numbers 1 to 7 become attached to these days.
I have yet a further proof to offer to you in support of what I have
said. Life as we understand is made up of mind and matter. Our thoughts
act arithmetically except in the case of those who are capable of mental
gymnastics and always think in terms of equations. The phenomena which
is the object of our thoughts obey geometrical laws which in other words
means logarithmical laws. What is the result? It is not easy to explain it.
The best and the easiest way in which I might convey to you this idea of
mind and matter acting on two different scales i.e., arithmetically and
logarithmically, is to refer you to two rows of nnmbers 159 and 139. The
first represents the arithmetical series and the second represents the log-
arithmical series. Any one who is acquainted with a slide rule and its pro-
perties will readily understand that 3 is the mid point on the scale of the
slide rule and 5 is the mid point of the 9 digits of our system of numerals..
These two coincident series 159 and 139 together totalling 28 really form
the circle of life enclosing within itself all the other numbers which you will
readily see are 234 678 in one case and 245678 in the other, the numbers

ence. Now there is a constant number attached to each circle irrespective
of its size and known as the value of Pi. This value is inseparable from any
circle whether its size be that of a point or the perimeter of the universe.
No matter what the circumference might be this value of Pi is there. Now
