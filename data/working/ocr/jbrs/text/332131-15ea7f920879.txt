The key and accompanying list include 233 wild
and cultivated trees and shrubs growing on the Univer-
sity Estate, Rangoon. It is by no means a complete
list of the higher vegetation of the Estate, since it has
been found best to leave out some of the still unidentified
trees. Where the key to uncertain ones has been worked
out, the herbarium specimen numbers are given.
The key is based on vegetative characters only,
primarily those of the leaves. At times the keying out
of a specimen is facilitated by the use of bark, stem,
habit, or habitat characters. The key is a purely artificial
one, no attempt having been made to reveal phylogenetic
relationships. This vegetative key will make possible
the identification of those plants throughout the year,
whereas a key based on floral characteristics is of use
only during the few days that a tree is in flower, or in
the herbarium.
Before this vegetative key could be produced, it was
necessary to collect and identify the flowers of all the
specimens included in order that the identification might
be certain; this part of the task required a full year.
Then the vegetative key was drawn up directly from these
identified specimens rather than from the literature. It
is hoped that future botany students in the University
and others interested in the rich vegetation of the
University Estate will find this key useful.
My thanks are due to U Aung Din, B. Sc. (Hons.)
B. A. (Oxon.) D. C. F., the Silviculturist, and U Khan,
Curator of the Forestry Department Herbarium for their
help in the identification of numerous specimens, and to
Dr. F. G. Dickason who has guided and encouraged me
in this study,
