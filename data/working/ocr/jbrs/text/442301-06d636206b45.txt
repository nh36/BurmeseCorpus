U BA BA - AN APPRECIATION
THE MEMBERS OF THE BURMA RESEARCH
SOCIETY are very grateful to U Ba Ba who served as a
part-time clerk of the Society from 1 April 1934 to 31
August 1959 and as a full-time clerk from 1 September
1959 to 31 January 1960 when the Society was busily
celebrating the Fiftieth Anniversary (28 Dec. 1959-2 Jan.
1960). He had done indispensable and invaluable.
services for the Society for nearly sixteen years. Owing
to his close intimacy with the affairs of the Society and
his willingness and efficiency, the business of the Society
prospered in many ways. All the members were truly
sorry when they came to know of his retirement at the
end of March 1960.
U Ba Ba was born of U Htan and Daw Ku on 3
September 1899 in Godwin Road, Iron Bazaar, Rangoon
and was an Old Paulian and Judsonian. We are also
proud to note that he was one of the 1920
boycotters. We all wish him a long life, health, peace
and prosperity.
