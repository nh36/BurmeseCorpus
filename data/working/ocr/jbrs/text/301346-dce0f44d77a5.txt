'How many fingers do I hold up?'-A correspondent in America
has written to the Society asking if there is any variant of the
children's guessing game commonly known in English-speaking
countries as 'How many horns has the buck?' or 'How many
fingers do I hold up?' He has discovered variants of it in no less
than twenty-five countries, including India and Turkey as well as
many European countries, and would be grateful for any information
as to its existence in Burma. He has forwarded the following
description of the game: -
"One player is blindfolded. Another, standing behind him,
strikes him repeatedly on the back, keeping time to a
rude sort of rhyme which he recites the while. At the
conclusion of the rhyme, the second player holds up a
number of fingers and calls upon the other
to guess
how many are held up. If the first player succeeds,
the rolls are reversed; if not, the game continues as before
until a correct guess is made.
In some variants the blindfolded player gets down on hands
and knees, and the other mounts upon his back. In
still others there are several participants. One stands
with his back against a tree or a wall, and holds his
hands clasped in front of him. Another faces him,
bending over until his face rests in the clasped hands of
the first. A third jumps upon the back of the second
and holds up a number of fingers, which the latter has
to guess. In this instance the first player's duty is to
act as a sort of umpire and to make sure that the second
cannot see.
