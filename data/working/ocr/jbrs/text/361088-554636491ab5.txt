Book Review by T. Ht.-
BOOK REVIEW
Southern Asia: Publications in Western Lan-
guages, A Quarterly Accessions List. Washington, The
Library of Congress, Division of Orientalia, 1952. Vol.
I. no. 1. IV. 56 p. 50 cents a copy.
.
Countries of Southern Asia are of current world
interest, and the appearance of this List is to be wel-
comed by students as well as librarians. The reading
materials listed therein include books, monographs and
selected articles from periodicals. The entries are fully
transcribed though annotations are not included. The
pattern of the catalogue is a very acceptable one with
appropriate subject-groups under each geographical region.
The lay-out is pleasing and typography easy to consult.
It may be assumed that the fourth quarterly issue of
the List will contain combined index to authors, titles
and subject-groups. A word of praise should be added
to Messrs. Cecil Hobbs, Walter H. Maurer and Charles
Byers of the South Asia Section of the Division of
Orientalia, Library of Congress, who have taken pains
to collect the material and present it in a most syste-
matic manner.
T. Ht.
