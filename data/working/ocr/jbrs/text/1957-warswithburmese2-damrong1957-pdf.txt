WAR No. 6
When the Siamese fought against the Governor of Bassein at
Muang Suphanburi. The year of the monkey,
Buddha Era 2127.
As has been stated before Somdet Phra Naresuon declared the independence of
Siam in the sixth Siamese month (June), year of the monkey, Chula Sakkaraj 946.
Buddha Era 2127. In that year in the 7th Siamese month (July) the army of Hongsawadi
entered Siam and attacked it. The subject matter of this war is clearly stated in the
Siamese history, as follows:-
When the King of Hongsawadi, Nanda Bayin, returned with his army from Ava
and reached his capital, he came to know that Somdet Phra Naresuon's army collected
Siamese families and took them away to Siam. Then he fought and drove away Nandasu
and Ratsankhram out of Siamese territory. His Majesty came to the conclusion that the
Siamese had revolted for certain. He therefore mobilised his army to attack the Siamese
capital.
At that time the King of Hongsawadi thought that the Siamese had suffered
depredation of their country only recently, and there would be not as many men to defend
the city as was the case formerly. It would not be necessary to send a very large army
containing many army corps as in the invasion of the King of Hongsawadi, Bayin Naung;

On the part of Somdet Phra Naresuon was already preparing for the impending
invasion, and watching the movements of the enemy. He dispatched reconnoitering
parties in all directions form which the enemy was likely to come. When he became
aware that the enemy was coming in two directions he made preparations to fight. He
raised a force of 10,000 volunteers, composed of the northern people under the command
of Chow Phya Sukhot'ai, and another force of volunteers, composed of the people in the
circle of the capital, manning the boats, under the command of Phya Chakri, with Phya
Khlang as second in command. These two forces were prepared to fight the enemy
immediately, wherever found. He adopted a scorched earth policy, making the people
either to collect and transfer all foodstuffs, or to destroy them along the two routes by
which the enemy was likely to come. He then collected all the people to enter the city,
and made preparations to defend the forts of the city as best he could.
It was probably due to underrating the power of Siam, or to the misunderstanding
between the governor of Bassein and the viceroy of Chiengmai in fixing the date to meet
together at the capital of Siam. One of them was at fault, with the consequence that two
Burmese forces did not arrive at the same time. In the first Siamese month (January) the
forces under the governor of Bassein entered Siamese territory and came by way of
Muang Kanchanaburi. There was only one force to contend with. Somdet Phra Naresuon,
on becoming aware of this, found a favourable opportunity, but at that time the waters
from the north had not all subsided, there was plenty of water in the river and canals, the
roads were rather wet, and the march of an army was not yet easy.

"Phraraj, you know, is equivalent to the Burmese Daw 6, Biti means Ceremony, Tat means to
Cut, Mai means Wood, Khom means to Press down, here means to Subdue and Nam means Name.
According to ancient principles and methods of warfare before an army leaves the Capital of a
Kingdom to meet the enemy's forces, a ceremony has to be performed to ensure success, and this ceremony is
what is known as "Biti Tat Mai Khom Nam " i.e." Cutting the wood which corresponds with the name of the
Enemy."

stalks are planted, at some distance apart form each other.

WAR NO.7
When there was a fight between the Viceroy of Chiengmai and the Siamese at Ban
Sraket. The year of the cock, Buddha Era 2128
As a matter of fact, this war was a forerunner of War No.8 which will be related
hereafter. But as it was fought separately and fighting ceased at the end of it. It was
counted as a separate war. From the time when the governor of Bassein and the viceroy
of Chiengmai suffered defeats at the hands of the Siamese in the year of the monkey.

home. The viceroy of Chiengmai was much afraid of the King of Hongsawadi, so he
went back and brought his forces down to Nakon Sawang in the year of the monkey, the

WAR NO.8
When the King of Hongsawadi invested the Siamese Capital.
They year of the dog, Buddha Era 2129.
This war was known a long time beforehand as in the case when His Majesty
King Bayin Naung asked for white elephants. Indeed, the forces of Maha Uparaja were in
occupation of Siamese territory, cultivating the fields since the year of the dog, Buddha
Era 2129. His Majesty King Nanda Bayin northern provinces to the enemy. His Majesty
the King of Hongsawadi gathered all his forces at Muang Kamp'engpet consisting of
250,000 men of all arms. He arranged to have 3 main royal armies, one of all which he
commanded himself, one was commanded by Maha Uparaja; the third and last army was

Somdet Phra Naresuon's method of waging war was different from that of the
Burmese in two important respects. Namely, he was an adept in resisting with a small
number of men against a vastly greater number of men. The Burmese were not like this.
When they tried to suppress the Siamese, they brought an army and when it was not
successful they brought an army the next year, and so on every year in succession,
without waiting for a favourable opportunity. But Somdet Phra Naresuon, when he
obtained a favourable opportunity, availed himself of it without letting it slip. When there
was not a favourable opportunity, he did not act, as when he attacked Muang Hongsawadi
the first time. Failing to obtain success in that expedition, he remained quiet for 3 years,
and when he got an opportunity he attacked Hongsawadi again.

The last war of Somdet Phra Naresuon.
Year of the major dragon, Buddha Era 2147.
The war waged by Somdet Phra Naresuon on this occasion did not arise from a
quarrel with the King of Hongsawadi as in previous occasions. It arose from the troubled
state of Burma, the news of which reached the kingdom of Siam. Therefore, it is deemed
necessary to explain what happened in Burma for the proper understanding of the subject
of this war.
The idea of viceroy of Toungoo in taking away the King of Hongsawadi to
Toungoo, as stated in War No.14, was to let the people know that he was acting for the
good of His Majesty of Hongsawadi, and to issue orders throughout the kingdom in the
name of His Majesty, thinking that if he was able to keep His Majesty in Toungoo he
would naturally inherit the kingdom. When Somdet Phra Naresuon retreated, the viceroy
of Toungoo sent letters in the name of the King of Hongsawadi to all governors of big
and small towns, that the last war had allowed the enemy to come very close to the royal
city of Hongsawadi, because according to the horoscope of Hongsawadi it was a time
when the city was in its decline, and unhappily many dangers arose at the time. His
Majesty consequently thought of staying in Toungoo and making it his capital for 7
years. When the evil star of Hongsawadi came to an end, he would go back and reside at
Hongsawadi as before. He asked the provinces great and small not to have any misgiving
on that account. One receipt of this royal proclamation some of the provincial governors
believed it, but there were a great many who did not believe it, and thought it was only a
ruse of the viceroy of Toungoo to wrest the sovereignty from the King of Hongsawadi.
Those governors who were still loyal to his Majesty, concerted together and marched
with their forces to Toungoo with a view to take away His Majesty by Force. The viceroy
of Toungoo came to know of the march of the provincial governors; he therefore told His
Majesty, and induced him to believe that the governors in question had become partisans
of Somdet Phra Naresuon and deceitfully thought of handing His Majesty over to the
Siamese King Somdet Phra Naresuon. He asked His Majesty to issue a royal order to

WAR No. 16
When the Burmese attacked Muang T'awai.
The year of the cow, Buddha Era 2156.
Somdet Phra Akat' otsarot ascended the throne in the year of the minor dragon,
Buddha Era 2148. At that time she Siamese were powerful to the fullest extent. Their
territories were more extensive than at any former time, and Siam was held in respect by
every foreign country surrounding it. Although Somdet Phra Naresuon had died, the
respect in which he was held by all people descended to Somdet Phra Akat'otsarot, as he
enjoyed the fame that he also was a fighter, having engaged in every war waged by
Somdet Phra Naresuon from the very beginning to the end. For this reason, when Somdet
Phra Akat' otsarot ascended the throne, there was no war and not a single enemy, external
or internal, who raised its head.
It is true that at that time Burma was in a dilapidated condition and divided into
several families or parties. It was possible to attack and get possession of Burma without
difficulty, if only Somdet Phra Akat'otsarot was fond of war like Somdet Phra Naresuon.
