The Society's Library.-Several up-country members of the
Society have inquired about the rules for borrowing books from the

meantime books may be loaned from the Library at the discretion
of the Librarian.

JOUR. BURMA RESEARCH SOC.
XXX, I, 1940.
Since 1930 the Library has been housed in the University Lib-
rary, the authorities of which have kindly set apart a room for that
purpose. Dwarfed and overshadowed by its illustrious host however,
the Library has not been very much used in recent years. A greater
interest on the part of members would probably result in it being
brought up to date and regular purchases being made.
At present there are 3,697 volumes in the Library. A catalogue
of these has been carefully compiled and will shortly be published
as a supplement to the Journal. This will also include a list of
the publications of other learned societies received by the Society.
