PALI & OLD MON INK GLOSSES IN PAGÁN TEMPLES
PREFACE
My readings of the glosses in the older temples of Pagán do not add
much to what is printed in translation in Vol. I of "Old Burma-Early Pagán”.
But they include the original text as read by my colleagues in Rangoon
University or the Burma Archaeological Department, as well as myself. I first
read many of these 30 to 40 years ago; and year by year they have been
flaking so very likely half are now no longer legible. I wonder if these
still remain after the earthquake? They are mostly either in Old Mon or Pali:
nearly all antedate 1150 A.D., when the official literary language at Pagán
changed from Old Mon to Old Burmese. Vast numbers of ink-glosses in Old
Burmese continue.
I have either published my readings of some of these, or lost them
during the war. I am now 87; and propose to send one copy each of xerox set
of my readings to some interested persons including Burma Archaeological
Survey for use as they see fit. It shows that it was addressed primarily to the
Archaeological Survey, as a debt of honour and gratitude to them for all
they did to help me and my colleagues in our field work at Pagán during
half-a-century.
I am delighted to learn from my old friend and colleague Saya Nai
Pan Hla that the Burma Research Society has accepted to publish my 130
odd pages of Pagán Ink-glosses as suggested by him. I thank the
President and members of the Society warmly for doing so, especially the
Editor for his effort in checking the proofs so carefully not to be in any loss
of accuracy or not to have printing errors.
In studying the history of Buddhism and literature of Old Burma,
it is vitally important for everything in Old Mon and Pali written in the
temples of Pagán. They must be recorded before it is too late. I am almost
blind. Reading and writing are difficult. I can do very little now.
My dear love and deepest gratitude are due to all the many friends.
and colleagues, living or dead, who have done so much to help me.
Blessings on you all!
Dec., 27, 1975.
Uplands, Grande Route,
St. Lawrence, Jersey,
Channel Islands, Gt. Britain.
Ba U.P.-N. 97-1,100-8-11-76;
B. S.
Gordon H. Luce
