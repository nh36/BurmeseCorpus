In the traditions attached to the Po-wun-daung in the Chindwin Valley

that the tribes under the monarchy at Prome or Srikshetra were the Pyu,
Thet and Kanran, and that a civil war arose among them. The Pyu fought
among themselves and separated into two parties. "One division having
occupied the hilly district to the south-east of Prome (i. e. Taungnyo), was
attacked by the Talaings, and then crossed the great river into the country
west of Padaung. There they were attacked by the Kanran as intruders
